AGRIBORA is an educational crop recommendation project. It uses soil nutrients and environmental measurements to make a model-based prediction from its training data.

The dataset includes 22 crop labels and seven input features. Its measurements reflect the dataset’s own units and collection context; confirm those units and the suitability of local measurements before relying on a result.
